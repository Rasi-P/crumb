Row 3 left to right: 9 strawberry cream white cake with fresh strawberries, pale pink background; 10 classic red velvet cake with white frosting and red crumbs, light stone background; 11 lavender tall anniversary cake with purple flowers, light lilac background; 12 buttercream cupcakes arranged in a tight group of 6 with sage and white frosting, light warm gray backdrop. ## Cake Studio scene assets

- `public/assets/flowers/{garden-rose,open-rose,peony}.glb`: original procedural curved-petal and leaf meshes authored for this repository. Lossless Meshopt compression, regenerated by `scripts/studio/build-assets.ts`. No externally sourced cake or flower model is included.
- `public/assets/flowers/rose.svg`: original vector library thumbnail.
- `public/assets/materials/cake-studio.hdr`: original procedurally authored Radiance RGBE studio lighting map with three softbox regions, built by the same script.
- `src/studio/assets/optimer_regular.typeface.json`: Three.js Optimer example typeface, original embedded license metadata retained. Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/fonts/optimer_regular.typeface.json
- Frosting micro-bump maps are generated deterministically in memory. No remote texture request or third-party texture license is needed.
- The supplied cake photographs guide proportions and material direction; they are not redistributed as application assets.

### Studio realism revision

- `public/assets/materials/studio-small-09.hdr`: **Studio Small 09**, Sergej Majboroda / Poly Haven, CC0. [Asset and license](https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09). Original 1K Radiance HDR downloaded from `https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/HDRIs/hdr/1k/studio_small_09_1k.hdr`. Served locally; no runtime external dependency. The earlier procedural HDR is retained as an authored asset but the renderer now uses the photographed studio.
- `public/assets/fonts/GreatVibes-Regular.ttf`: **Great Vibes**, Robert Leuschke / The Great Vibes Pro Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1. [Source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/greatvibes); full copyright and license in `public/assets/fonts/OFL.txt`. The Latin subset in `src/studio/assets/great-vibes.typeface.json` is converted locally by `scripts/studio/build-font.ts`, preserving license metadata. Both 2D lettering and extruded 3D lettering use this font.
- Revised flower GLBs remain original procedural assets, with individually cupped petals, rolled asymmetric edges, root color variation and sculpted leaf veins. They are not photogrammetry scans. Petal micro-bump and object-space buttercream shading are authored in `scene/materials.tsx`.
