Each tile is square, equal-sized, no gutters or borders, no words or labels. Whole image aspect ratio 4:3. All cakes photographed at the same elegant slightly elevated 3/4 angle, each entire cake and base visible with generous empty space, cake takes 65% of each tile. Natural soft studio daylight, sharp edible buttercream textures, understated pale neutral seamless backdrops in differing hues. Row 2 left to right: 5 minimal white birthday cake with one thin elegant candle and little daisies, pale white background; 6 pastel sky blue baby shower cake with tiny cloud sugar motifs, light blue background; 7 striking black and gold round luxury cake with gold leaf accents, light gray background; 8 rainbow pastel buttercream kids birthday cake, pale mint background. Row 3 left to right: 9 strawberry cream white cake with fresh strawberries, pale pink background; 10 classic red velvet cake with white frosting and red crumbs, light stone background; 11 lavender tall anniversary cake with purple flowers, light lilac background; 12 buttercream cupcakes arranged in a tight group of 6 with sage and white frosting, light warm gray backdrop. Premium independent bakery editorial product photography.
